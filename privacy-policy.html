Privacy Policy

ทำนายฝัน Auto Post

Effective date: 18 September 2026

In short This app is a single-operator content-publishing tool. It is used only by its own developer to publish that developer's own video content to that developer's own TikTok and Facebook accounts. It is not a public-facing service, does not accept sign-ups from other users, and does not collect data about anyone else.

01Who operates this app

ทำนายฝัน Auto Post is a personal automation tool built and operated by the developer of the "ทำนายฝัน" content channel. It connects to the developer's own TikTok account (via the TikTok Content Posting API) and the developer's own Facebook Page, solely to publish short-form video content that the developer has already produced.

02What data this app accesses

The app accesses only the following, and only for the developer's own connected accounts:

  • Authentication tokens — an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok (and, separately, a Page access token issued by Facebook), used to authenticate publishing requests.
  • Basic account identifiers — the connected TikTok user ID and Facebook Page ID, used only to address the correct account when publishing.
  • Video files and captions — the video content and caption text that the developer supplies to be posted. This content is produced entirely by the developer beforehand; the app does not read, store, or analyze any other content on the connected accounts.

The app does not access follower lists, direct messages, analytics data, or any content belonging to other TikTok or Facebook users. It has no login flow, account-creation flow, or data-collection form for the public.

03How this data is stored and used

Tokens and identifiers are stored locally on the developer's own machine (in a local configuration file), never on a shared or public server, and are never transmitted anywhere other than directly to the TikTok and Facebook APIs to authorize a publish request. Video files are stored locally until they are uploaded and are not retained by any third party beyond TikTok's and Facebook's own standard hosting of published posts.

04Data sharing

This app does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. It does not use data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than publishing the developer's own videos to the developer's own accounts.

05Data retention and revocation

Access tokens remain valid until revoked. The developer can revoke this app's access at any time from TikTok's account settings (Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or from Facebook's Business Integrations settings, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

06Changes to this policy

Because this is a single-operator tool, this page is updated directly by the developer whenever the app's data practices change. The effective date above reflects the most recent update.

07Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the "ทำนายฝัน" channel's official contact channels.
